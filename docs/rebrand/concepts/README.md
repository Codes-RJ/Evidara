## Shared prompt brief

Create independent premium production-quality desktop research UI mockups. Preserve access to literature search, stance synthesis, comparison, citation graphs/context, PDF upload/chat, equation explanation, BYOK settings, and Markdown/CSV/JSON/BibTeX export. Prioritize one clear research workflow per screen. Use readable typography, realistic spacing, and restrained separation; avoid feature inventories, nested cards, device mockups, watermarks, and invented certainty scores. Use the research question “How does retrieval improve language-model accuracy?” as sample context. Anchor visible current dates to 2026-09-27. Treat the legacy screenshot as context rather than a style to clone.

## Direction prompts

**Evidara / Evidence Studio:** A clean white/cool-gray research workspace with navy text and cobalt actions. A compact project sidebar, broad question input and Search papers action, followed by an evidence overview and selected-paper list. Show stance counts, abstract-based analysis, source links, and a highlighted cited excerpt. Communicate traceability with a contemporary sans-serif visual system and generous whitespace.

**Tracefolio / Research Atlas:** A dark charcoal spatial workspace with muted lavender/cyan accents and horizontal navigation. Make a sparse citation network the dominant surface, with a selected-paper inspector showing abstract, provenance, and citation context. Provide an Explore papers action and quiet contextual research tools. Avoid neon, glow, heavy gradients, and decorative sci-fi network imagery.

**Studyloom / Editorial Research Desk:** A warm off-white reading-first workspace with charcoal ink, forest-green actions, restrained ochre highlighting, serif editorial headings, and sans-serif controls. Use a split paper reader and assistant/notebook. Show a highlighted source passage, cited answer, source excerpts, an evidence note, and Create evidence brief as the primary action. Preserve scholarly character without the old volume/issue decoration.

The owner selected option 1, Evidara / Evidence Studio, for implementation. Options 2 and 3 remain alternatives. Original generated images are preserved; PNGs here are continuity copies.
